## Responsive and interaction choices
Above 800px use the quiet product header; at intermediate widths suppress the optional Features shortcut before crowding the brand. At 800px and below show an icon-only three-line menu and a full-viewport modal, Offers first. Escape/close restores focus; in-page navigation moves focus to the destination; lock background scroll while open. Stack feature/media/story pairs and platform actions. Gallery controls sit below the image, wrap visibly on compact views and keep the page free of horizontal overflow; the image opens at full size. No hover-only essential actions. Touch/mobile devices see a desktop explanation. A validated download is the primary action itself for a known desktop platform. The Mac archive is universal; never guess Apple CPU architecture. Mobile, ChromeOS, known unsupported ARM desktops and unknown devices get labelled choices. Missing or untrusted assets never become direct downloads. Show the same-origin manifest before reconciling GitHub metadata so an external lookup cannot delay a known download.
