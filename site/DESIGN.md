## Job and direction
A canvas for everything. A free, open-source visual workspace for ideas, diagrams, interfaces, whiteboards and banners, usable alone or with a coding agent. Primary action: a clearly labelled desktop download. The page has its own neutral product identity; family relationships remain in Offers and the footer.

## Real content and limits
All five captures use the new shared native workspace from frozen PR9 source 1b04c31, rendered on Apple M4/Metal. They are development previews; current main-branch packaging prototypes do not contain that UI yet. The site itself is an SVG design study imported into the real canvas with desktop/mobile compositions, editable vector layers and cropped native screenshot image layers, not a running HTML page. The banner is the owner-selected real LinkedIn artwork as an image layer, surrounded by editable vectors. Remaining samples are editable public illustrative diagrams, notes and layout studies. Exact source/fixture/binary hashes are in assets-sources.json. Never present these as a publicly released app before qualification.

## Responsive and interaction choices
Above800px use the quiet product header; at intermediate widths suppress the optional Features shortcut before crowding the brand. At 800px and below show an icon-only three-line menu and a full-viewport modal, Offers first. Escape/close restores focus; in-page navigation moves focus to the destination; lock background scroll while open. Stack feature/media/story pairs and platform actions. Gallery buttons wrap on narrow views; image opens at full size. No hover-only essential actions. Touch/mobile devices see a desktop explanation. OS detection leads to labelled architecture choices, never an assumed Apple Silicon installer.

## Acceptance
Inspect narrow/intermediate/wide layouts, both themes, relevant keyboard/menu states, actual files and all gallery choices. Check no accidental horizontal page overflow, adequate contrast and readable captions. The preview is not hardware certification. Preserve the source/upstream licences and individual brand ownership. Keep local ignored downloads out of deployment by exporting committed files with scripts/export-product-site.py. Domain/indexing/public downloads stay held until the exact latest-UI app and deployed destination are qualified.
